
# AI-Assisted Financial Analysis Report

## Company

Reliance Industries Limited

## Analysis Period

FY2023-24 to FY2024-25

## Financial Performance

Revenue increased from ₹10,00,122 crore in FY2023-24
to ₹10,71,174 crore in FY2024-25.

Net profit increased from ₹79,020 crore in FY2023-24
to ₹81,309 crore in FY2024-25.

## Financial Analysis

The analysis calculates:

- Revenue Growth
- Net Profit Growth
- Net Profit Margin
- Current Ratio
- Debt-to-Equity Ratio

## Key Strengths

- Revenue increased year-on-year.
- Net profit increased year-on-year.
- The company showed positive overall growth.

## Areas to Monitor

- Future revenue and profit growth
- Liquidity position
- Debt levels
- Long-term financial performance

## Overall Assessment

Based on the selected financial indicators, Reliance Industries Limited
showed positive revenue and net profit growth between FY2023-24 and FY2024-25.

This project demonstrates how Python and AI-assisted analysis can be
used to convert financial data into structured business insights.
